01 / OVERVIEW

Gameplay understanding, across horizons.

GameHorizon Suite studies how models follow and understand natural-language instructions during real gameplay — from fine-grained operations to mid-level goals and long-horizon missions — across diverse AAA titles with human keyboard-and-mouse action traces, evaluated through a reproducible offline track and a stepwise online track.
