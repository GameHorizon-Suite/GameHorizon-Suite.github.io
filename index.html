01 / OVERVIEW

Gameplay understanding, across horizons.

GameHorizon Suite studies how well models follow and understand natural-language instructions during real gameplay — from fine-grained operations to mid-level goals and long-horizon missions. It spans diverse AAA titles with human keyboard-and-mouse action traces, and evaluates models through a reproducible offline track (deterministic multiple-choice) and a stepwise online track that localizes failures within long-horizon tasks.
